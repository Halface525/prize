# Charter of the Halface Prize

(Made September 2026)

## Chapter 1 — General Provisions

**Article 1 (Name)** This prize is named the Halface Prize, 「半面奖」 in Chinese, hereafter "the Prize."

**Article 2 (Establishment)** The Prize is established by halface as an individual. The Prize is affiliated with no government, company, university, political party, religious body or international organisation, and receives funding or direction from none of them.

**Article 3 (Purpose)** The Prize rewards the developer in the internet industry with the lowest rate of artificial-intelligence use; the university student with the least screen time; the person who has eaten in the canteens of the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China more times than anyone else; and any person, animal, artificial intelligence or object that displays an equally interesting quality in daily life.

**Article 4 (Character)** The Prize is an honour. It does not assess the merits or demerits of artificial intelligence, of electronic devices, or of canteen food, and constitutes no academic, professional or moral assessment of any kind.

> Note: The purpose of this article is to ensure that the Prize cannot be cited as evidence by any party. Anyone citing the Prize to argue that artificial intelligence is harmful, that electronic devices are harmful, or that canteen food is good, has misused it.

**Article 5 (The Committee)** The Prize has a Selection Committee, which is its sole adjudicating body. The Committee decides for itself how many members it has, and need not publish its reasons.

**Article 6 (Powers of the Committee)** The Committee may create awards, classify applications, determine laureates, declare vacancies, and construe this Charter. The Committee is under no obligation to give reasons for any decision.

## Chapter 2 — The Prize

**Article 7 (Source)** The prize money comes entirely from the personal savings of halface.

**Article 8 (No donations)** The Prize accepts no donation, sponsorship, gift, matching contribution or financial support of any kind. The Prize maintains no channel for receiving funds. Any fundraising conducted under the name of the Halface Prize has nothing to do with the Prize.

**Article 9 (Amount)** The total prize for each drawing equals the entire cash balance of the prize account at the moment of the drawing. There is neither a floor nor a ceiling.

**Article 10 (Division)** The prize for a drawing is divided equally among all laureates of that drawing. Where a drawing has exactly one laureate, that laureate receives the entire balance.

**Article 11 (The zero principle)** The Prize accumulates no capital, holds no permanent endowment and conducts no investment. After every drawing the prize account returns to zero.

> Note: The prizes that already exist commonly accumulate capital and pay out from its interest. This Prize does the opposite. A rising balance is not the purpose of the Prize; that the balance returns to zero after every drawing is its normal state and not a malfunction.

**Article 12 (Nil balance)** Where the account balance is zero at the time of a drawing, the drawing proceeds as normal, the prize is zero, and the result is published as normal.

**Article 13 (Number of laureates and individual shares)** The more laureates in a drawing, the less each receives. This follows necessarily from the rule of equal division and is not a defect of the Prize.

> Note: The Prize neither encourages nor forbids an applicant to discourage others in order to increase their own share. The Committee reserves the power, where it finds that several people have applied on the same grounds, to confer the award on all of them together.

## Chapter 3 — Domains

**Article 14 (Domains)** The Prize has seven domains:

| No. | Domain | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | The Body | Sleep, diet, exercise, health |
| II | The Mind | Learning, memory, attention, thought |
| III | Labor | Occupation, work, craft, use of tools |
| IV | Relations | Roommates, friends, family, strangers |
| V | Expression | Speech, writing, publishing, silence |
| VI | Objects | Possession, use, endurance, loss |
| VII | The Non-Human | Animals, plants, machines, artificial intelligences |

**Article 15 (Status of the domains)** The domains are set at the level of this Charter. They may not be added to, removed, merged, split or renamed except by the amendment procedure of Chapter 7.

**Article 16 (Individual awards)** Each domain contains individual awards. Individual awards are unrestricted and are created freely by the Committee at each drawing.

**Article 17 (The award library)** An award once created enters the award library and may be reused in later drawings. An award once in the library may not be withdrawn — withdrawal would leave past drawings without foundation.

**Article 18 (Classification)** An applicant need not name a domain. The Committee assigns the domain.

**Article 19 (Residual domain)** Anything that fits none of the seven domains listed in Article 14 falls into the eighth domain, "The Useless."

> Note: The sole reason this domain exists is so that the seven domains listed in Article 14 never have to change.

## Chapter 4 — Application

**Article 20 (By application)** The Prize is awarded by application. Sending an application email to the Committee's address completes the application.

**Article 21 (Free of charge)** The Prize charges no fee, and no applicant is asked to pay postage, materials, review costs, a deposit or any other sum.

**Article 22 (Eligibility)** Anyone may apply, without restriction as to nationality, age, education, occupation, health or place of residence. Applications for animals, objects and artificial intelligences are made by a person acquainted with them.

**Article 23 (Repeated applications)** The same grounds may be submitted in different drawings. The number of applications is neither an advantage nor a disadvantage.

**Article 24 (Self-declared figures)** There is no third-party audit. Figures reported by an applicant are asserted by the applicant; the Committee does not verify them and bears no duty to verify them.

**Article 25 (Withdrawal)** An applicant may withdraw at any time before the drawing. Withdrawal is not possible after the drawing — by then the record has entered the archive.

## Chapter 5 — Adjudication

**Article 26 (Cycle)** The Prize is adjudicated once a week, beginning after the A-share market closes on Friday.

**Article 27 (Time zone)** All times referred to in this Charter are Beijing time (UTC+8).

**Article 28 (Number of laureates)** There is no limit on the number of laureates per drawing. There may be one, there may be several, there may be none.

**Article 29 (Vacancy)** Where a drawing receives no application, or none sufficient to constitute grounds for an award, the Committee may declare that drawing "vacant." A vacancy is numbered in sequence and is neither postponed nor merged. The vacancy is itself the result of that drawing.

**Article 30 (Own initiative)** Where no application has been made, the Committee may confer an award on the basis of public information.

**Article 31 (Grounds)** The Committee writes the grounds for each laureate. The grounds shall be concrete, plain, and free of evaluative ornament.

## Chapter 6 — Publication

**Article 32 (What is published)** After each drawing the Committee publishes on the Prize's website: the number of the drawing, its date, the names of the laureates, their domains, the awards conferred, the grounds, the total prize, the amount received by each laureate, and the balance of the account after the drawing.

**Article 33 (No amendment)** A published record is not amended. Where an error has genuinely occurred it is listed separately as an erratum, without overwriting the original.

**Article 34 (Names)** A laureate is published under the name given in the application. Where an applicant asks to be published under a pseudonym only, the Committee does not disclose their identity.

**Article 35 (Objections)** Anyone may write to object to a result. The Committee is under no duty to reply, but reserves the right to correct clear errors of fact.

## Chapter 7 — Final Provisions

**Article 36 (Amendment)** This Charter is amended by the Committee. An amendment does not apply retroactively to drawings already published.

**Article 37 (Construction)** The interpretation of this Charter rests with the Committee. The Committee's understanding may change over time.

**Article 38 (Entry into force)** This Charter takes effect on the day of its publication.
