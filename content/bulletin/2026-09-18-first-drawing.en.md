Notice of the Halface Prize Selection Committee

The first drawing of the Prize will begin on Friday 18 September 2026 at 15:00 Beijing time, after the A-share market closes.

As of the publication of this notice the balance of the prize account is ¥1,247.30. That sum is the total prize for this drawing, divided equally among its laureates. The account returns to zero afterwards.

There is no limit on the number of laureates. There may be one, there may be several, there may be none.

Where a drawing receives no application, or none sufficient to constitute grounds for an award, the Committee will declare that drawing "vacant." A vacancy is numbered in sequence and is neither postponed nor merged. The vacancy is itself the result of that drawing.

Applications should be sent to halface16@gmail.com. They are free of charge, in any format and any language. The Committee does not reject an application on grounds of format.

The result of this drawing will be published on the Prize's website once adjudication is complete.
