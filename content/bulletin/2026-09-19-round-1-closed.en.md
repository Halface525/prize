Notice of the Halface Prize Selection Committee

The first round of the Prize closed on 19 September 2026.

By the close of adjudication the Committee had received no application.

Under Article 30 of the Charter, where no application has been made the Committee may confer an award on the basis of public information. The Committee has accordingly conferred one award this round.

The result of this round is published under "Laureates" on the Prize's website; this notice does not repeat it. Under Article 33, a published record is not amended.

Under Article 11, the prize account returned to zero when this round was drawn.

Round 2 opens on Friday 25 September 2026 at 15:00 Beijing time, after the A-share market closes.

Applications should be sent to halface16@gmail.com. They are free of charge, in any format and any language. The Committee does not reject an application on grounds of format.
