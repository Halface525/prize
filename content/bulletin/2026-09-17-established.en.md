Notice of the Halface Prize Selection Committee

The Halface Prize is hereby established, and takes effect on the day of this notice.

The Prize is established by halface as an individual. It is affiliated with no government, company, university, political party, religious body or international organisation, and receives funding or direction from none of them.

The Prize rewards the developer in the internet industry with the lowest rate of artificial-intelligence use; the university student with the least screen time; the person who has eaten in the canteens of the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China more times than anyone else; and any person, animal, artificial intelligence or object that displays an equally interesting quality in daily life.

The prize money comes entirely from the personal savings of halface. The Prize accepts no donation from any source and maintains no channel for receiving funds. It accumulates no capital and conducts no investment; the account returns to zero after every drawing.

The Prize has a Selection Committee, which presently has one member. The Committee decides for itself whether that number rises or falls.

The full Charter of the Halface Prize is published on the Prize's website. Its interpretation rests with the Committee.
