Notice of the Halface Prize Selection Committee

Article 5 of the Charter of the Halface Prize was amended on 19 September 2026, with effect from the day of this notice.

Before:

> The Prize has a Selection Committee, which is its sole adjudicating body. The Committee presently has one member. The Committee decides for itself whether that number rises or falls, and need not publish its reasons.

After:

> The Prize has a Selection Committee, which is its sole adjudicating body. The Committee decides for itself how many members it has, and need not publish its reasons.

Reason for the amendment: a charter is a durable document, and should not carry a number that changes.

Under Article 36, this amendment does not apply retroactively to drawings already published.

The amended Charter is published on the Prize's website, as is an outline of the Committee.
