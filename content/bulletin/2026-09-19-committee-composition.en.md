Notice of the Halface Prize Selection Committee

The Prize's Selection Committee now has three members.

On 18 September 2026 the Committee issued letters of appointment to two candidates. Both took office on 19 September, for an indefinite term.

The two new members are published as:

- Anonymous
- Hajixian

Both have asked to be published under a pseudonym. Under Article 34, the Prize does not disclose their identities.

Under Article 5, the Committee decides for itself how many members it has, and need not publish its reasons. This notice is issued to record the change in its composition.

The list of members and an outline of the Committee are published on the Prize's website.
