Round 7 of the Prize opened at 15:00 on Friday 30 October 2026, after the A-share market closed. By the close of adjudication the Committee had received no application sufficient to constitute grounds for an award.

Under Article 29 of the Charter, where a drawing receives no application, or none sufficient to constitute grounds for an award, the Committee may declare that drawing "vacant." A vacancy is numbered in sequence and is neither postponed nor merged. The vacancy is itself the result of that drawing.

The Committee accordingly declares: this drawing is vacant.

### Committee's note

This round was due to be published on 31 October 2026 and was published on 2 November, two days late. Under Article 26, delay does not alter the numbering of rounds.

At the time of this drawing the balance of the prize account was zero. Under Article 12, where the balance is zero at the time of a drawing, the drawing proceeds as normal and the prize is zero — so had anyone won this round, the prize would likewise have been zero.

These are two independent facts: no laureate this round, and no prize money this round. Each has its own cause.

Under Article 11, the Prize accumulates no capital and conducts no investment. The balance has stood at zero since round 1, and no further deposit has been made.

### Settlement

No laureate this round, and no prize paid out. The prize for round 8 is counted afresh from the next deposit.
