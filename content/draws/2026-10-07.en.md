The third drawing of the Prize opened on Friday 2 October 2026 at 15:00 Beijing time, after the A-share market closed. By the close of adjudication the Committee had received no application.

Under Article 30 of the Charter, where no application has been made the Committee may confer an award on the basis of public information. The Committee does so.

The account published a headline on 6 October 2026 at 18:29: 《刚刚，诺贝尔物理奖一人独揽！拼多多创始人母校教授独享》 — "Just now, one person takes the Nobel Prize in Physics alone! A professor at the Pinduoduo founder's alma mater takes it alone."

The headline states three things: that the Nobel Prize in Physics was announced that day; that one person took it alone; and that the laureate's university is the alma mater of the founder of Pinduoduo.

The Committee notes that the third of these has nothing to do with the conferral of that prize.

The Committee found nothing in the headline that is untrue. The award is conferred accordingly.

### Committee's note

This drawing should have opened on 2 October 2026. Its result is published on 7 October, five days late.

The reason for the delay is that none of the Committee's three members remembered it.

Under Article 26, the Prize holds one drawing each week. The delay does not change the numbering; the fourth drawing still runs from Friday 9 October 2026.

The Committee finds nothing improper in this.
