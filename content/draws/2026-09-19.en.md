The Prize was established on 17 September 2026, and its first round opened at 15:00 on 18 September. By the close of adjudication the Committee had received no application.

Under Article 30 of the Charter, where no application has been made the Committee may confer an award on the basis of public information. The Committee does so.

This person established the Halface Prize in September 2026 and appointed himself its sole Committee member. The post pays nothing, carries no budget and has no funds to draw on — under Article 7 the prize money comes entirely from this person's personal savings, and under Article 8 the Prize accepts no donation from any source. In other words, holding the post brings in no income and requires the holder to put money in.

The Committee notes that this person has received no remuneration from the post. The figure published above is that fact. Under Article 24, the Prize conducts no third-party audit.

The Committee holds that this cannot be optimised, cannot be compared, and cannot be put on a CV. The award is conferred accordingly.

### Committee's note

The laureate of this drawing is the founder of the Prize, and the sum comes from the founder's own savings. The Committee finds nothing improper in this.

Under Article 11, the account returns to zero after the drawing. The prize for round 2 is counted afresh from the next deposit.
