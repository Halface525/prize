No application was received this round. Under Article 30 of the Charter, where no application is received, the Committee may make an award on its own initiative, on the basis of public information. The Committee so awards.

Round 6 of the Prize is awarded to "A Study of Turn-Taking, Emoji and Topic Half-Life: A 401-Day Complete Case Study of a Three-Person Group Chat," Journal of This Group Chat, vol. 1, no. 1, October 2026, by BAN Mian, HA Jixian and NI Mingzhe.

The sample is one complete export of a group chat: three members, 5,611 messages, 401 days. Nothing was sampled; every message was coded. Thirteen were skipped whole for privacy, while their timestamps and message types were kept, so that totals and time series remain intact.

By the paper's count, 56.2% of codable messages are short responses, emoji/images or quotes; the median block-to-block reply latency is 16 seconds, with 77.1% of replies arriving within one minute; a median episode runs 7 messages over 4.7 minutes.

What these figures describe is a group chat whose main activity is not conversation but confirmation that the others are still there.

The Committee therefore takes what is rewarded this round to be not a paper but the measurement of a capacity: the capacity to hand a topic over to a single reply, and to feel no loss in doing so.

The group chat described in the paper is the group chat to which its authors belong. The Committee found nothing improper in this. The award is made accordingly.
