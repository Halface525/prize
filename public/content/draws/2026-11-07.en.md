Round 8 of the Prize opened on Friday 6 November 2026 at 15:00 Beijing time, after the A-share market closed. By the close of the round, one application had been received.

Under Article 16, specific awards are created freely by the Committee at each drawing. The Committee creates this round the Meta Application Prize, and makes it to that application. The laureate is Dog King.

The Prize published its Charter on 17 September 2026. From that day to the opening of this round, seven drawings were held. In those seven rounds the Committee received no application at all.

Under Article 20, an application is complete when an email is sent to the Committee's address. That provision has stood there since the day it was published, unaltered. For seven rounds, nobody used it. In round 8 it was used for the first time.

"Meta" here is not an adjective. The object of the Meta Application Prize is the level of application itself — and the prize must itself be applied for in order to be won. A thing whose object is itself can happen only once. The first application now exists; the meta level is therefore closed. A second application will not win it, nor a third. The award was created this round and will not be given again.

This round is not without anything else to award, and this prize is not a placeholder. This round did carry one concrete application, as follows: a battery used from January to October 2026, which did not swell.

Under the definition of the Never Swollen Prize, the award goes to "a battery that completed a full service life without ever swelling." At the opening of this round that battery was still in service; its service life was not complete. The Committee therefore does not make that award this round. Under Article 24 the Committee does not verify the data an applicant reports; what the Committee relies on here is not data but time — the battery has been in use for less than a year, and the full service life of a battery is usually counted in years.

The award made this round is not compensation for the one that was not. Under Article 23, the same matter may be applied for again in a different round. If the battery is still unswollen when its service life ends, it may be applied for again, and will then be assessed under the Never Swollen Prize.

One thing is over. The other is not. What is over can be judged; what is not, cannot.

The Committee therefore takes what is rewarded this round to be a single act of submission: after 47 days, seven drawings and no applications at all, someone followed the published rules, finished what they asked for, and sent it. The act yields nothing and alters no one's circumstances. Its only effect is to make this Prize, for the first time, a thing that can be applied for.
