### First award: Least Reliance on AI Prize

**Laureate**: A person who prefers not to be named
**Domain**: III. Labor
**Published figure**: Rate of AI tool use, 0%

Grounds:

Asked by the Committee, they said the practice arose not from any objection to artificial intelligence, but because "I type quite fast — by the time it has finished thinking, I have finished writing."
